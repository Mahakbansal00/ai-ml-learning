 Once upon a time, there was a little star named Twinkle. ⭐
Twinkle wanted to shine brighter than all the other stars in the sky. But one night, dark clouds covered the sky, and Twinkle disappeared behind them. Twinkle felt sad.
Then the Moon said, “Twinkle, your light may be small, but it is special to someone.”
After some time, the clouds moved away, and Twinkle began to shine again. A little child on Earth looked up at the sky and smiled. 🌙✨
Twinkle realized that even a little light could brighten someone's world.
Moral: Never underestimate your own worth. 💖
